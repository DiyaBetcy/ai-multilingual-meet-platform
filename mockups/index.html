Design system · proposal

Obsidian Intelligence

A warm-forest, emerald and champagne identity for the AI-Powered Multilingual Meeting Platform. These are static, isolated mockups of the three surfaces named in the brief — dashboard, authentication and the live meeting room — built only from data the application already owns. No file under frontend/vite-project was touched.

Dashboard mockup Authentication mockup Meeting room mockup
Foundation

Obsidian ground, forest depth

The app sits on #101713. Surfaces climb through #18221C, #202D25 and #26372D so hierarchy is carried by luminance, not by stacked shadows and borders.

Accent

Emerald acts, champagne annotates

Emerald #35D39A is reserved for interactive and genuinely active states. Champagne #D8C6A1 appears only on premium metadata — timers, the AI badge, selected meeting labels — never as a second CTA colour.

Type

One scale, no shouting

A 11 → 32 px scale on the already-installed system sans stack. Restrained weights, no oversized headings, uppercase reserved for short eyebrows and status labels.
